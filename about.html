Welcome to the Studio Ghibli Spirits Gallery

Main characters like Totoro, Chihiro, and Howl get most of the attention and merchandise. But when watching Studio Ghibli movies, the background creatures and familiars are often the most fun and charming part. This gallery was created to give the spotlight to those lesser-known, cute side characters—like the giant duck spirits relaxing in the bathhouse, Boh turned into a mouse, and the little white spirit puffs waiting to be born.

Data & Attribution

The spirits featured in this gallery come from various Studio Ghibli films directed by Hayao Miyazaki, including Spirited Away, My Neighbor Totoro, Howl's Moving Castle, Ponyo, and The Boy and the Heron. All character lore, roles, and descriptions are based on their appearances in these animated films. Images are used for non-commercial student project and educational demonstration purposes.
